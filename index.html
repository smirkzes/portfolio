about me.

Hello, im Antonia/smirkz, whatever you want to call me, a digital artist based in germany.

I've been drawing actively for roughly 7-8 years now.

I started out with traditional work but soon found out that there is a lot more freedom when it comes to digital art compared to traditional.

I really enjoy studying anatomy and 3d space, im fascinated in composition.

I've been looking more into shading, rendering, color theory and backgrounds as of recently.

I am detail oriented and really open minded when it comes to art and life itself, i would definitely love to learn and improve upon my artistic works.
